# Third-party preset sources

The preserved source snapshots under `tools/presets/` retain data from [v2fly](https://github.com/v2fly/domain-list-community), [hxehex](https://github.com/hxehex/russia-mobile-internet-whitelist), and [chebur](https://github.com/chebur-net/russia-mobile-whitelist), under their [MIT notices](LICENSES/). Published releases include detailed per-preset source attribution.

`tools/presets/compatibility.json` retains 54 records from the [KeenProxy v0.9.3 catalog](https://github.com/ordo-systems/keen-proxy/blob/v0.9.3/internal/catalog/presets.json). Its original collection provenance and redistribution terms are not established. The upstream license notices and this repository's software license do not establish rights for this historical subset.
